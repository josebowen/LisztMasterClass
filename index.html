An Afternoon with Liszt

Three afternoons a week, pupils gathered in Liszt's music room in Weimar. He charged nothing. He rarely told anyone how to play a passage. He spoke in images.

Walk in. Click a person or the piano. Read what Liszt said, in his pupils' words, and hear recordings two of those pupils made decades later.

Sound: five short clips from discs made in 1912 and 1922.
