# Sources checked for this build (13 Nov 2026)

## Quotations
All quotations in `src/content.js` marked `via: 'bowen'` are copied from the quotations in
Bowen, "Liszt the Teacher," JALS 52/53 (2002/03), with the page citations given in its footnotes
(notes 124, 142, 177, 179, 182, 186, 189, 190, 196, 198, 199, 201, 209, 213, 227).
https://weteachwithai.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Bowen-Liszt-the-Teacher-Journal-of-the-American-Liszt-Society.pdf

Read directly in the source scans (OCR text, Liszt_Teaching_Sources/PDFs):
- Fay, Music-Study in Germany (1883 printing): Au bord d'une source passage ("wood-chopper", "poetical little end"); "don't make omelette".
- Siloti, "Meine Erinnerungen an Franz Liszt," ZIMG 14 (1913): the "Moonlight" passage (Steckenpferd; the G sharp; third movement only begun; small carpeted room, 35 to 40 people, worn piano). English is a new translation for this demo. NOT yet checked by José.

Not found in the folder: Göllerich diary (Zimdars), Lachmund (Walker), Ramann Liszt-Pädagogium. Lachmund wording comes only through the article.
