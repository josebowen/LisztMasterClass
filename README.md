# An Afternoon with Liszt

A short demo for faculty workshops. Pick a topic and read what Liszt said, in his pupils' words, beside photographs of the people who reported it. Then hear clips from discs two of those pupils made in 1912 and 1922.

Live: https://josebowen.github.io/LisztMasterClass/ . More demos: https://weteachwithai.com/vibe-coding/

- Research: José Antonio Bowen, "Liszt the Teacher," JALS 52/53 (2002/03). See `data/sources-verified.md`.
- Photographs load from Wikimedia Commons (Louis Held group photograph; portraits of Lamond, d'Albert, Fay, Mason). Credits are in the About panel.
- Clips in `clips/` are cut from Internet Archive transfers: Lamond, HMV D 718-719 (1922); d'Albert, Odeon (1912).
- Build: `npm i && node build.mjs`. Test: `node test/walk.mjs`.
